## See it in action

<div class="rp-card">

**StreamHub** noticed nearly 1 in 5 visitors abandoned checkout right after seeing the price. They added an Abandon Cart prompt that reappears in the nav bar after two minutes, with a small discount attached — and recovered roughly a third of those carts within a month.

</div>

<div class="rp-card">

When a **FitTrack Pro** member clicks Cancel, an LTV-based offer appears instantly — a bigger discount for long-time members, a smaller one for newer sign-ups. Save-flow retention roughly doubled once the offer matched the customer's actual value instead of a single flat discount.

</div>

<div class="rp-card">

An anonymous reader on **NewsDaily** who returns three times in a week sees a soft prompt: enter your email for early access to tomorrow's top story. It's converted more casual visitors into registered readers than any exit-survey pop-up they tried before.

</div>
