# Definition

<div class="rp-definition">Expiring content notifications are automatic email alerts sent when a prompt or guide is about to expire. You choose who receives them and how often.</div>

# Key benefits

<div class="rp-benefits">
  <div class="rp-benefit">
    <div class="rp-benefit-icon"><i class="fa-solid fa-bell" aria-hidden="true"></i></div>
    <strong>Proactive content management</strong>
    <span>Receive automatic email alerts when a prompt or guide is about to expire, so you can stay ahead of content changes.</span>
  </div>
  <div class="rp-benefit">
    <div class="rp-benefit-icon"><i class="fa-solid fa-shield-halved" aria-hidden="true"></i></div>
    <strong>Uninterrupted user experience</strong>
    <span>Avoid unintended service interruptions and content gaps by knowing exactly when to update or replace your content.</span>
  </div>
  <div class="rp-benefit">
    <div class="rp-benefit-icon"><i class="fa-solid fa-arrow-trend-up" aria-hidden="true"></i></div>
    <strong>Protected performance</strong>
    <span>Get notified about expiring content that may be crucial to user engagement, so you can act and maintain performance.</span>
  </div>
</div>

# Key details

To enable email notifications for expiring prompts and guides, follow these steps.

<div class="rp-steps">
  <div class="rp-step">
    <div class="rp-step-num">1</div>
    <div><h4>Confirm your Recurly Engage access</h4><p>Make sure you're an active Recurly Engage member. If you're not, <a href="https://recurly.com/product/engage/" target="_blank">book a demo</a>.</p></div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="rp-steps">
  <div class="rp-step">
    <div class="rp-step-num">2</div>
    <div><h4>Open notification settings</h4><p>In Pulse, the Recurly Engage management console, navigate to <span style={{fontWeight: "bold"}}>Settings &gt; App Management &gt; Notifications</span>.</p></div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="rp-steps">
  <div class="rp-step">
    <div class="rp-step-num">3</div>
    <div><h4>Adjust your preferences</h4><p>Adjust your email notification preferences and recipients for expiring prompts and guides.</p></div>
  </div>
</div>
