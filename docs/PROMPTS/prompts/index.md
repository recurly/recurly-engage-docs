# Definition

<div class="rp-definition">A prompt is an in-app message that appears when a user visits your application or website. It consists of a headline, body copy, background, and up to three call-to-action buttons. Recurly Engage supports multiple prompt types — overlay and inline — across desktop, mobile, tablet, and TV devices.</div>

## Prompt types

Recurly Engage supports a variety of in-app message formats, all designed in accordance with the <a href="https://www.iab.com/guidelines/iab-new-ad-portfolio/" target="_blank">IAB Guidelines</a>:

<div class="rp-nav-grid">

<Cards>
  <Card title="Inlines" href="https://docs.recurly.com/recurly-engage/docs/inlines" target="_blank">
    Prompts embedded directly in your page layout, in horizontal, vertical, tile, or text-only formats.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Overlays" href="https://docs.recurly.com/recurly-engage/docs/overlays" target="_blank">
    Floating prompts that appear above your content, including notification, interstitial, pop-up, video, and bottom banner formats.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Invisible" href="https://docs.recurly.com/recurly-engage/docs/invisible" target="_blank">
    Silent prompts delivered through tracking pixels or JavaScript callbacks for analytics.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Push" href="https://docs.recurly.com/recurly-engage/docs/push-notification" target="_blank">
    Scheduled notifications sent to users' devices (ADM, APNs, FCM) to drive re-engagement.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Video" href="https://docs.recurly.com/recurly-engage/docs/video" target="_blank">
    Rich-media overlays that play video with text and call-to-action buttons in a pop-up modal.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Mobile interstitial" href="https://docs.recurly.com/recurly-engage/docs/mobile-interstitial" target="_blank">
    Full-screen native prompts on iOS and Android, rendered through the SDK for immersive mobile experiences.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## How prompts work

A prompt can be targeted to one or more <a href="https://docs.recurly.com/recurly-engage/docs/segments" target="_blank">Segments</a> and set to <a href="https://docs.recurly.com/recurly-engage/docs/triggers" target="_blank">Trigger</a> on events like button clicks, page visits, or time spent on a page. You can <a href="https://docs.recurly.com/recurly-engage/docs/schedule-1" target="_blank">Schedule</a> prompts for specific dates and times and <a href="https://docs.recurly.com/recurly-engage/docs/limits" target="_blank">Limit</a> their delivery by user count or budget. For use-case inspiration, explore our <a href="https://docs.recurly.com/recurly-engage/docs/popular-uses" target="_blank">Popular use cases</a>.

You can also run A/B tests with our built-in <a href="https://docs.recurly.com/recurly-engage/docs/experiments-1" target="_blank">Experiments</a>, helping you choose the most effective variation before a full rollout.

## The Prompts page

The Prompts page gives you a quick view of every prompt in your account, showing its status, design preview, target segments, schedule, and high-level performance metrics (impressions, clicks, and click-through rate). Use the **Search** bar or the filters — Status, Device/type, and Segment — to find what you need.
