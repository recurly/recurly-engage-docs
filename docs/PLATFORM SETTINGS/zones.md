### Prerequisites

<ul class="rp-list">
  <li>You must have <span style={{fontWeight: "bold"}}>Company</span> or <span style={{fontWeight: "bold"}}>App Administrator</span> permissions in Engage.</li>
</ul>

# Definition

<div class="rp-definition">A <span style={{fontWeight: "bold"}}>zone</span> is a named placeholder in your application or website where Engage can insert prompts or perform invisible actions. Zones blend into your UI to guide users, display promotional content, or trigger behaviors such as hiding ads for VIP users.</div>

# Key benefits

<div class="rp-benefits">
  <div class="rp-benefit">
    <div class="rp-benefit-icon"><i class="fa-solid fa-circle-check" aria-hidden="true"></i></div>
    <strong>Flexible placement</strong>
    <span>Define zones anywhere in your layout — top banners, sidebars, tiles, or inline sections.</span>
  </div>
  <div class="rp-benefit">
    <div class="rp-benefit-icon"><i class="fa-solid fa-circle-check" aria-hidden="true"></i></div>
    <strong>Multiple modes</strong>
    <span>Render text-only, display items, or invisible actions without user-facing UI.</span>
  </div>
  <div class="rp-benefit">
    <div class="rp-benefit-icon"><i class="fa-solid fa-circle-check" aria-hidden="true"></i></div>
    <strong>Dynamic targeting</strong>
    <span>Combine with segments, triggers, and limits to serve context-sensitive content.</span>
  </div>
</div>

# Key details

You can deliver one or more prompt items into a zone. When multiple items are present, you can choose **Stack**, **List**, or **Slider** display modes.
