# How can I download prompt activity data?

You can download summary metrics or detailed, event-level activity data as CSV files.

## Metrics data

You can download summary metrics for one or more prompts in either of two ways.

### From the Prompts screen

On the **Prompts** screen, select your date range, check the prompts you want, click **Export to CSV**, and then choose **Summary**.


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/2cec8af-image.png" align="center" width="75%" border={true} />


### From a prompt’s detail screen

From a prompt’s **Manage** menu on its detail screen, choose **Export to CSV**.


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/733eebf-image.png" align="center" width="75%" border={true} />


## Detailed activity data

For a full event-level export:
