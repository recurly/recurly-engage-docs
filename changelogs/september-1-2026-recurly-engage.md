September 1, 2026 Release: This release adds a new way to target subscribers by their payment method, so you can tailor prompts based on how someone pays — alongside a small display fix in Engage Admin's scheduling field.

| Release Date | **Feature**         | **Type**    | **Potential Impact** | **Description / Overview**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| :----------- | :------------------ | :---------- | :------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sept 1       | **User Traits**     | Enhancement | Med                  | You can now target and segment by a new `payment_method_type` trait, based on each subscriber's primary payment method on file. No action is required to use it — it's available now for new segment and prompt conditions.                                                            |
| Sept 1       | **Engage Admin UI** | Bug Fix     | Low                  | Fixed a display bug where the Daypart scheduling field showed a raw number instead of the configured time range (e.g. "10am–5pm"). This was a display-only issue — schedules were applying correctly. Also fixed a stray vertical bar that could appear in Engage admin's text fields. |
