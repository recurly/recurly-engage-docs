The July 21 release introduces three major new capabilities to Recurly Engage — Figma Sync, a Template Library, and guided Tours — alongside targeted enhancements to connector actions and mobile pop-up layouts, plus a fix for Android background image rendering.

| Release Date | **Feature**                                      | **Type**    | **Potential Impact** | **Description / Overview**                                                                                                                                              |
| :----------- | :----------------------------------------------- | :---------- | :------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Jul 21       | **Figma Sync**                                   | New         | High                 | Import a Figma design directly into a Web or Mobile Pop-Up and Engage builds a styled prompt automatically, no manual CSS.                                              |
| Jul 21       | **Template Library**                             | New         | High                 | Start new prompts and guides from use-case templates that pre-fill name, description, segment, and styling.                                                             |
| Jul 21       | **Tours**                                        | New         | High                 | Build multi-step, tooltip-based guided walkthroughs anchored to elements on your site to onboard new subscribers — a native prompt type with built-in Engage analytics. |
| Jul 21       | **Subscription Targeting for Connector Actions** | Enhancement | Medium               | Set a plan code to target a specific subscription for pause, resume, cancel, or reactivate actions.                                                                     |
| Jul 21       | **Native Pop-Up Button Layout**                  | Enhancement | Medium               | Android and iOS pop-up modals now support vertically stacked, full-width buttons. Rolling out gradually.                                                                |
| Jul 21       | **Android Pop-Up Background Images**             | Bug Fix     | Medium               | Fixed background images not rendering on Android phone and tablet pop-ups.                                                                                              |
